# Reviewer Qualification & Due Diligence

**Status:** Operational checklist proposal — no reviewer is currently represented as appointed or approved.

## 1. Minimum checks for every reviewer

- [ ] Legal name and identity verified through a reliable channel.
- [ ] Organization and role independently verified.
- [ ] Relevant current qualification, registration, licence or practising status verified with the competent professional body or authority, where applicable.
- [ ] Scope-specific Myanmar experience demonstrated (company law, tax, accounting, audit, software security, etc.).
- [ ] Independence confirmed: no undisclosed financial, family, employment, ownership or other material conflict.
- [ ] Conflicts and any limits on independence disclosed in writing.
- [ ] Scope, deliverables, assumptions, exclusions and reliance limitations agreed in writing.
- [ ] Fee, timetable and revision process agreed.
- [ ] Confidentiality, data minimization, secure transfer, retention and deletion terms agreed.
- [ ] Written report and traceable sign-off agreed before work begins.

## 2. Legal reviewer due diligence

Verify the individual's current authority and professional standing through the relevant Myanmar authority or professional mechanism. Determine whether the proposed work requires an Advocate, Higher Grade Pleader, law firm, or another specifically qualified professional. Do not assume titles are interchangeable. Ask for evidence of current entitlement to practise and relevant Myanmar corporate, tax, commercial and data/privacy experience.

## 3. Accounting and tax reviewer due diligence

Verify membership/registration and current practising status directly with the Myanmar Accountancy Council or other competent professional authority, as applicable to the service. Confirm the distinction between CPA qualification, public-practice registration and any audit-specific authorization. Request relevant Myanmar tax and accounting experience, including the relevant entity type and tax periods.

## 4. Technical reviewer due diligence

Request a portfolio or verifiable history of relevant work in secure mobile applications, database/ledger design, data migration, threat modelling, software supply-chain security and reproducible CI. Require review of the exact commit and reproducible evidence, not only a verbal opinion.

## 5. Independence and conflict questions

Each reviewer should answer:
1. Do you own, control, develop, sell or receive compensation from RZ Profit or a competing product?
2. Do you have a personal, family, employment or financial relationship with the Founder or project contributors?
3. Are you being paid contingent on a PASS, launch, tax outcome or transaction?
4. Have you previously authored or approved the material you are reviewing?
5. Are there other circumstances that could reasonably affect impartiality?

A disclosed conflict does not automatically disqualify a reviewer, but the Founder must document the assessment and mitigation. Material unresolved conflicts mean the review is not independent.

## 6. Acceptable deliverable

The report must identify reviewer and qualifications, exact scope and exclusions, artifact/commit reviewed, evidence examined, findings and severity, unresolved issues, conclusion, limitations, date and signed/traceable confirmation. A generic email saying “looks good” is insufficient for material legal or tax reliance.

## 7. Candidate sourcing and status

Potential reviewers may be sourced through official professional bodies, official registers/directories, reputable firms and referrals. Discovery is not appointment. A listed person or firm is not presumed available, independent, qualified for the exact scope or willing to review. Verify current status directly before engagement.

## 8. Data handling

Share only the minimum information needed. Redact customer personal data, bank details, credentials, tax identifiers and confidential commercial information unless strictly necessary and lawfully shared under agreed safeguards. Prefer synthetic test data for technical review. Record where data is stored, who can access it, retention period and deletion confirmation.
