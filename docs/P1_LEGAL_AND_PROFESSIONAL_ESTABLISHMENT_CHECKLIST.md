# P1 Legal & Professional Establishment Checklist — Myanmar

**Status:** Planning and evidence checklist only. Not a legal opinion, registration instruction, professional licence determination or authorization to provide regulated services. Every legal conclusion must be confirmed by Myanmar counsel and the relevant authority or professional body before reliance.

**Parent foundation:** `docs/MASTER_ESTABLISHMENT_BLUEPRINT.md`  
**Related controls:** `docs/LEGAL_AND_REGULATORY_REGISTER.md`, `docs/SECURITY_PRIVACY_AND_THREAT_MODEL.md`, `docs/MYANMAR_TAX_RULES_GOVERNANCE.md`  
**Research baseline:** 12 November 2026. Re-check all laws, forms, fees, portals and procedures on the date of action.

---

## 1. How to use this checklist

For each item, record:

- **Status:** `NOT_STARTED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `BLOCKED`, `PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW`, `VERIFIED`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
- **Owner:** founder, company secretary/registration agent, Myanmar lawyer, qualified accountant/tax practitioner, security/privacy adviser or other named responsible person.
- **Evidence:** official source, signed decision, application/receipt, certificate, written professional opinion, policy or approved template.
- **Verified on:** date checked against the current authority/source.
- **Reviewer:** independent reviewer and their relevant qualification/authority.
- **Next action / due date:** explicit action and target date.

A tick is not a legal conclusion. Mark an item `VERIFIED` only when the underlying evidence has been reviewed and its applicability confirmed. Do not enter invented registration numbers, licences, approvals or professional credentials.

## 2. Founder decisions — resolve before selecting a registration route

- [ ] **Operating model:** decide whether the first release is (A) software-only, (B) software plus bookkeeping/accounting/tax-support services, or (C) software plus a network/referral model using independently engaged professionals.
- [ ] **Initial customer:** confirm the initial target segment (for example, Myanmar owner-managed micro/small businesses) and explicitly defer complex groups, cross-border cases or regulated engagements if not ready.
- [ ] **Initial service catalogue:** approve each proposed service separately: software subscription, bookkeeping, transaction processing, management reporting, accounting preparation, tax workpaper preparation, tax-return assistance, tax advice, audit, legal advice, representation before authorities and dispute/appeal support.
- [ ] **Service exclusions:** identify services that RZ Profit itself will not provide unless a written legal/professional opinion confirms the required authority and competence.
- [ ] **Geography:** decide whether operations and customers are Myanmar-only initially; identify any overseas customer, data-hosting, payment or contractor relationships.
- [ ] **Ownership and control:** document founders, beneficial owners, directors/partners, decision rights, funding and conflicts of interest.
- [ ] **Brand claims:** approve accurate product and service descriptions. Do not use terms such as audit firm, CPA firm, licensed tax agent, legal practice, certified or officially approved unless the exact claim is verified and authorized.
- [ ] **Risk appetite:** approve the rule that no tax computation, legal interpretation, statutory filing or professional opinion is released without the required competent review.

## 3. Legal form and entity establishment

Do not assume a private company is automatically the best or only structure. Compare the proposed business activities, ownership, liability, tax treatment, reporting, cost and governance consequences for each legally available form.

- [ ] Obtain written advice comparing the relevant choices, which may include sole proprietorship, partnership, company or another permitted structure, as applicable to the actual ownership and activities.
- [ ] Confirm whether the intended entity can lawfully conduct each proposed software, bookkeeping, accounting, tax-support, consultancy or referral activity.
- [ ] Confirm foreign ownership, residency, director, shareholder and investment restrictions if any founder, investor or customer arrangement raises those issues.
- [ ] Confirm the exact registering authority, current application route, required forms, identity documents, constitutional documents, fees and processing steps from current official material.
- [ ] If company incorporation is selected, obtain current DICA/MyCO requirements and professional advice on name, company type, constitution, directors, members, registered office, share structure and beneficial ownership disclosures.
- [ ] Confirm whether any separate sector, local, municipal, professional or activity-specific permit is required; do not infer that incorporation alone authorizes every service.
- [ ] Complete registration only after founder approval and legal review; retain submitted application, official receipt, certificate, constitution and current company extract.
- [ ] Establish a controlled register for registration details, annual/periodic returns, changes of directors or members, registered-office updates and other applicable statutory events.
- [ ] Obtain a written applicability assessment for company financial records, financial statements, audit, member/director approvals, annual return and retention obligations. Record exemptions only when the exact current provision and eligibility are confirmed.
- [ ] Create a calendar for each verified obligation, with source provision, responsible owner, due-date calculation, reminder and completion evidence.

**Evidence required:** signed legal-form comparison; official registration certificate/extract where applicable; constitutional documents; ownership/beneficial ownership records where required; official filings and receipts; counsel's written applicability note.

## 4. Professional authority, competence and service boundaries

This section is a mandatory gate. The checklist does not determine who is entitled to use a professional title, sign a report, conduct an audit, prepare or certify accounts, advise on tax, or represent a taxpayer.

- [ ] Ask Myanmar counsel and the relevant professional body to identify current laws, rules, notifications, membership requirements, practising certificates, firm registration/approval and restrictions applicable to every proposed service.
- [ ] Confirm the current Myanmar Accountancy Council (MAC) framework and the applicable distinction between an accountant, professional accountant, certified public accountant, practising accountant, audit practitioner and audit firm, using current primary rules.
- [ ] Verify the current status and scope of any proposed CPA, ACCA or other professional qualification, membership, practising entitlement and local recognition. A qualification or membership alone must not be assumed to confer every Myanmar statutory practice right.
- [ ] Obtain written advice on whether bookkeeping, accounting preparation, tax-return preparation, tax advice, audit, assurance, certification, legal advice or authority representation require distinct qualifications, registration, licence, practising certificate or firm approval.
- [ ] Confirm who may sign or issue financial statements, audit/assurance reports, tax opinions, tax returns, objections, appeals and formal representations, and under what engagement and authorization.
- [ ] Confirm whether the business entity itself must be registered/approved as a professional practice or audit firm, separately from an individual's qualification.
- [ ] Establish a competence matrix for each service, identifying minimum qualifications, experience, continuing professional development, supervision and escalation requirements.
- [ ] Prohibit staff from presenting themselves using protected or potentially misleading titles until entitlement is verified.
- [ ] Define a referral process for audit, legal advice, complex tax interpretation, litigation, statutory representation and other work outside RZ Profit's confirmed authority.
- [ ] Verify the external professional's identity, qualifications, current membership/practising status, scope, conflicts and engagement terms before referral or appointment.
- [ ] Obtain professional indemnity insurance advice and confirm whether insurance is required or prudent for the selected activities.
- [ ] Maintain a register of professional credentials, renewal dates, scope limitations, complaints, disciplinary restrictions where lawfully checkable, and verification evidence.

**No-go rule:** until this review is complete, RZ Profit may describe planned capabilities and provide general software functionality, but must not claim to be an authorized audit/accounting/tax practice or offer a service whose authority is unverified.

## 5. Tax registration and statutory obligations

- [ ] Obtain advice on which tax registrations apply to the selected legal form, actual activities, turnover, employees, locations and transactions.
- [ ] Confirm TIN registration and the current IRD process, documents, responsible taxpayer and authorized representative.
- [ ] Determine applicable tax types and return/payment obligations individually; do not assume all entities have the same income tax, commercial tax, specific goods tax, withholding or other obligations.
- [ ] Confirm the applicable tax year, accounting period, registration thresholds, commencement rules, filing frequency, payment method and effective dates from current primary authority.
- [ ] Confirm payroll, employer, withholding and employee-related obligations if staff are engaged.
- [ ] Confirm whether any municipal, local, industry or activity-specific tax/permit obligations apply to the actual operating locations and services.
- [ ] Set up separate records for returns filed, assessments, notices, payments, withholding certificates, credits, refunds, objections and appeals.
- [ ] Require a qualified reviewer to approve the entity-specific tax obligation matrix before configuring reminders or tax rules in production.
- [ ] Do not activate any rate, exemption, deduction, depreciation method, relief or deadline in the software solely from secondary commentary or an unverified research note.

**Evidence required:** IRD registration evidence; official forms/instructions; current primary-law references; written entity-specific tax applicability matrix; professional approval record.

## 6. Accounting framework and financial reporting

- [ ] Obtain a current written assessment of the accounting framework applicable to the selected entity type and reporting period.
- [ ] Obtain the original MAC notification, order or other primary instrument for any reported standards adoption or transition, and verify issue date, effective date, entity scope, exclusions and transitional provisions.
- [ ] Do not rely on the 2019 IFRS Foundation Myanmar profile or secondary summaries to settle 2026 applicability.
- [ ] Decide the initial chart of accounts, accounting policies, fiscal calendar, functional currency and financial statement presentation with a qualified accountant.
- [ ] Define responsibility for bookkeeping, review, period close, financial statement preparation and any statutory audit/assurance appointment.
- [ ] Confirm financial-record retention and audit requirements by legal form and applicable period.
- [ ] Document that management-prepared or accountant-prepared statements are not an independent audit opinion unless an authorized independent audit engagement has been performed.

## 7. Client engagement, conduct and service operations

- [ ] Prepare an engagement letter for each service category, with client identity, scope, deliverables, exclusions, period, fee, payment terms, responsibilities, assumptions and termination.
- [ ] State clearly whether RZ Profit is acting as software provider, bookkeeper, preparer, adviser, coordinator or referral channel in each engagement.
- [ ] Identify the named preparer, reviewer, client approver and any external specialist.
- [ ] Define client responsibility for completeness and accuracy of records, prompt disclosure, approval of adjustments and authorization of filings.
- [ ] Obtain informed consent for handling financial records and any optional cloud/OCR/AI processing.
- [ ] Include confidentiality, data access, retention, return/export, deletion, incident notification and subcontractor terms reviewed by counsel.
- [ ] Set a written fee schedule, invoicing process, refund/cancellation policy and approval for out-of-scope work.
- [ ] Establish complaint handling, correction, escalation, service-level and conflict-of-interest procedures.
- [ ] Define how errors are corrected, who is notified, how amended work is reviewed and how evidence is retained.
- [ ] Prohibit guaranteed tax savings, guaranteed assessment outcomes or claims that an authority will accept a proposed treatment.
- [ ] Require client authorization and competent professional review before any return, payment instruction, objection, appeal or representation is submitted.

## 8. Banking, finance, people and operational controls

- [ ] Open and operate business bank/payment accounts in the correct legal name where required; document authorized signatories and dual-control arrangements.
- [ ] Separate business and personal funds; define expense approval, reimbursement, petty cash, procurement and month-end reconciliation.
- [ ] Establish payroll, employment contracts, contractor agreements, workplace policies and applicable employer registrations after employment-law review.
- [ ] Confirm any work-permit, immigration, foreign-worker or contractor requirements where relevant.
- [ ] Define staff confidentiality, acceptable-use, access termination, device security and conflict declarations.
- [ ] Maintain accounting records for RZ Profit itself independently from client books.
- [ ] Set up business continuity, incident response, backup and disaster-recovery responsibilities.
- [ ] Establish vendor due diligence and written agreements for hosting, OCR, AI, messaging, payments, document storage and professional referrals.

## 9. Privacy, cybersecurity and client-data governance

- [ ] Obtain Myanmar counsel's current assessment of applicable cybersecurity, electronic-transactions, sectoral, contractual and any other data-related requirements for the actual operating model.
- [ ] Confirm whether any notification, registration, security control, incident reporting, data-localization or cross-border transfer requirement applies; do not assume a general privacy-law position resolves all duties.
- [ ] Create a data inventory covering TINs, identity documents, bank data, payroll, invoices, contracts, tax returns, client communications and access logs.
- [ ] Define purpose, lawful basis/authority as advised, access roles, retention period, deletion/hold rules and client rights/processes for each category.
- [ ] Keep financial documents on-device by default where feasible; cloud processing must be opt-in with provider, purpose, region, retention and deletion limits disclosed.
- [ ] Review hosting and subprocessors, access locations, international transfers, encryption, key management, breach handling and contractual safeguards before use.
- [ ] Implement least privilege, MFA for privileged access, organization separation, audit logs, encrypted backups and tested restore.
- [ ] Define a security incident process with containment, evidence preservation, counsel escalation and notification only as required by verified law.
- [ ] Complete a security/privacy review before any pilot involving real client financial data.

## 10. Tax-source and professional-quality governance

- [ ] Establish an official-source register with instrument number, issuing authority, publication date, commencement date, relevant period, section/page, captured source hash and last verification date.
- [ ] Separate primary law, official guidance, professional commentary, news leads and unverified reports.
- [ ] Assign a qualified reviewer to each tax type and accounting framework.
- [ ] Require source capture, applicability analysis, exception/transition review, worked examples, boundary/regression tests and independent approval before a rule becomes active.
- [ ] Retain the exact rule version, source hash, inputs, rounding method, assumptions and reviewer for each calculation.
- [ ] Use `POTENTIAL_DISCREPANCY` and `REVIEW_REQUIRED` for unconfirmed matters; do not label an amount as overpaid or a relief as approved until supported.
- [ ] Maintain a legal and professional change-monitoring process, with impact assessment, effective-date handling, test updates and rollback.

## 12. Launch gates and evidence dossier

### Gate A — Founder scope approval
- [ ] Operating model, customer segment, geography and service catalogue approved.
- [ ] Prohibited services and marketing claims documented.
- [ ] Legal form selected subject to counsel confirmation.

### Gate B — Legal establishment
- [ ] Entity registration and any required activity-specific permissions evidenced.
- [ ] Tax registration and entity-specific statutory obligation matrix verified.
- [ ] Current reporting, recordkeeping and filing obligations assigned.

### Gate C — Professional authority
- [ ] Written service-by-service authority and competence opinion received.
- [ ] Required individual and firm credentials verified.
- [ ] Signing, audit, advice and representation boundaries documented.
- [ ] Independent reviewer/referral arrangements in place.

### Gate D — Operating and data readiness
- [ ] Engagement, privacy, consent and complaint templates approved.
- [ ] Banking, staff, vendor and internal-control procedures ready.
- [ ] Security, backup/restore, incident and retention controls tested.
- [ ] No real client data used before security/privacy approval.

### Gate E — Controlled pilot
- [ ] Pilot scope and participating clients approved.
- [ ] Named qualified preparer and independent reviewer assigned.
- [ ] No unverified tax rule active; all outputs marked draft until reviewed.
- [ ] Client authorization and professional review completed before any external submission.
- [ ] Pilot findings, incidents, corrections and approvals documented.

**Launch is blocked if any mandatory gate is incomplete.** A completed checklist does not itself grant a licence, professional status or authority.
